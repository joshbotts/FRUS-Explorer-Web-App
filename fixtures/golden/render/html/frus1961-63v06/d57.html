57. Message From Chairman Khrushchev to President Kennedy

Moscow, October 4, 1962.

Esteemed Mr. President, On behalf of the Soviet people and myself personally, I am happy to congratulate you and the American people on the occasion of the successful completion of the flight of the space ship “Sigma-7” with Cosmonaut W. Schirra. Please convey cordial greetings and very best wishes to Cosmonaut-Airman Walter Schirra.

N. Khrushchev
