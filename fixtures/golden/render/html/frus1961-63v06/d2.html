2. Message From President-elect Kennedy to Chairman Khrushchev

November 10, 1960.

I am most appreciative of your courtesy in sending me a message of congratulations. The achievement of a just and lasting peace will remain a fundamental goal of this nation and a major task of its President. I am most pleased to have your good wishes at this time.
