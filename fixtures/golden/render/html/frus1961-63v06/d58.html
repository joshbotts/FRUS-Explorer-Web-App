58. Telegram From the Department of State to the Embassy in the Soviet Union

Washington, October 6, 1962, 12:25 p.m.

826. Following message for delivery soonest to Khrushchev from the President:

Begin Verbatim Text:

Dear Mr. Chairman:

I thank you and the Soviet people on behalf of the American people for your congratulations on Commander Schirra’s successful space flight. Your greetings and best wishes have been conveyed to Commander Schirra.

John F. Kennedy.

End Verbatim Text.

Time of release of this message by White House not decided.

Ball
