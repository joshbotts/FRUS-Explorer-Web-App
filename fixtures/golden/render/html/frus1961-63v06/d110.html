110. Message From Chairman Khrushchev to President Kennedy

Moscow, July 8, 1963.

Dear Mr. President: I thank you for the felicitations in connection with the successful protracted flight of Cosmonaut Valeri Bykovski and the first of its kind in the world for a woman—Cosmonaut Valentina Tereshkova.

The Soviet people are proud of the successes of their cosmonauts, breaking the trail into unknown expanses of the universe, and consider that cosmic space should be a zone of peace, a field for cooperation among nations.

N. Khrushchev
