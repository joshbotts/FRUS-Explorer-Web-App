16. Letter From President Kennedy to Chairman Khrushchev

Washington, June 10, 1961.

Dear Mr. Chairman: Many thanks for your kindness in presenting me with a case of beverages during our recent meeting in Vienna. I would also like to express my appreciation to the Soviet Government for the gold cigar chest, caviar and records. For these courtesies I am very grateful.

Sincerely,

John F. Kennedy
