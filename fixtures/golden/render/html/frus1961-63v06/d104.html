104. Message From President Kennedy to Chairman Khrushchev

Washington, May 29, 1963.

Dear Mr. Chairman: I am happy to inform you that the date of July 28, 1963, which you suggest for the arrival of Secretary Rusk in Moscow, is satisfactory. I have asked Secretary Rusk to get in touch with Minister Gromyko well in advance of this date in order to work out the arrangements, including a public announcement at an appropriate agreed time.

Source: Department of State, Presidential Correspondence: Lot 77 D 163. No classification marking. Another copy is in the Kennedy Library, National Security Files, Countries Series, USSR, Khrushchev Correspondence.
