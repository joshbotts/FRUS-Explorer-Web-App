No. 28.
Mr. Gresham to Mr. Baker.

Department of State,
Washington, April 2, 1894.

Sir: I have received your two dispatches of the 9th ultimo, relative to the disturbances at the Mosquito Reservation, stating that this Government has not failed to take the action which appeared proper to the end of protecting American interests there,

I am, etc.,

W. Q. Gresham.
