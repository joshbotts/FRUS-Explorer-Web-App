No. 109.
Mr. Gresham to Mr. Baker.

[Telegram.]

Washington, September 24, 1894.

Nicaraguan minister says Wiltbank and Lampton will be allowed, on request, to return to Bluefields to wind up business. Have they property or families at Bluefields or in Mosquito? In what business were they before July 5? Communicate with them without delay and report.
