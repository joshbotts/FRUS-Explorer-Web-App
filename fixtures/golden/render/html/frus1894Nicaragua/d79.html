No. 79.
Mr. Baker to Mr. Gresham.

[Telegram.]

Legation of the United States,
Managua, July 20, 1894. (Received July 22.)

Uprising of Indians and Jamaica negroes at Bluefields extremely unfortunate. They restored Chief Clarence and set up government. Presence of United States marines prevents further bloodshed. Minister Madriz goes down and will take letters from me advising Americans of their duty to the sovereign Government.
