No. 1.
Mr. Seat to Mr. Gresham.

[Telegram.]

Bluefields, Nicaragua, January 25, 1894.

Honduras invading at Cape Gracias. Lives and millions property American citizens unprotected. Appeal for man-of-war to Blueflelds Mosquito Reservation, at once.

Seat, Consular Agent,
(and fifty citizens of the United States).
